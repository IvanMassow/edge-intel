Methodology

How we test our predictions

1
Detect narrative pressure
Each day before US markets open, our engine reads hundreds of media, policy and supply-chain sources to measure sentiment pressure building on specific commodities.
2
Publish our call
We publish a directional call — for example, “Natural gas: sentiment rising” or “Oil: downward pressure on airlines.” Timestamped and locked before any market move.
3
Track the outcome
We measure using liquid ETF benchmarks. If the commodity moved >0.5% in the direction we called, that’s confirmed. We also measure lead time — how far ahead we detected the move.
