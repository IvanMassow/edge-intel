How It Works

NOAH’s signal engine extracts directional beliefs from narrative intelligence reports. Each belief is matched against live Kalshi contracts using semantic similarity. When our house probability diverges from the market price, we flag an edge in percentage points (pp).

Match confidence reflects how well the belief maps to the contract question. Higher confidence means a tighter semantic fit. Candidates with strong integrity signals and large edge gaps will be promoted to live trades once the system exits testing phase.

Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated exchange. All data shown here is from our signal engine — not financial advice.
