About

Born as a wire.
Built for intelligence.

The Origin

We started with a simple question

What if news could be genuinely useful? Not recycled. Not thin rewrites. Not yesterday's story repackaged. What if you could build news content so good that it doesn't just fill a page—it grows an audience?

That's how Noah Wire started. A wire service that actually works. Authoritative. Original. Copyright-clean. Every story fact-checked. Every piece carrying genuine editorial judgement.

The results were extraordinary. Publishers using our wires saw traffic grow by 200% in two months. Within six months, 700%. At a time when Google had cut visitor numbers to most publications by 40%.

The Evolution

From wire to intelligence

When you process every breaking story, every day, across every vertical—you start to see patterns. Not just what's happening, but what's about to happen.

We called it narrative signal analysis. The structural pattern beneath the headlines that reveals where events are heading before the market catches on. We built Bionic Ear—15 specialist configurations for traders, analysts, insurers, strategists, and governments.

Then we went further. Agent Noah is our fully autonomous investigative agent. It doesn't just find news. It hunts it. Council meetings, social feeds, public records, corporate filings. Proper investigative journalism, powered by AI.

Ethics

Intelligence with integrity

Every story we produce is fact-checked. Every claim is sourced. We publish fact-check results alongside our content—including when they fail. Transparency is not optional.

We don't manufacture news. We don't optimise for engagement over truth. We don't produce content designed to mislead. Our wire exists to make publications better, not to replace journalism with automation.

Our intelligence products are designed to inform decisions, not to make them. The human is always in the loop. The machine provides the signal. The person decides what to do with it.
