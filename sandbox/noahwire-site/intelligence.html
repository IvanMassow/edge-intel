Narrative Signal Analysis

If you can see it breaking, you can see what's next

We process every breaking story through our signal engine. Not keywords. Not sentiment. Narrative signal—the structural pattern beneath the headlines that reveals where markets, policy, and public opinion are heading.

We identify consensus signals, frontier signals, and discovery signals. Each carries a different edge window and confidence profile. The system doesn't just tell you what happened. It tells you what's about to.

Signal Taxonomy

Three types of edge

Consensus
Strong evidence, mirrors opinion
When the evidence aligns with current market thinking but the price hasn't moved yet. Shorter edge window—typically 6–12 hours. High confidence.
Frontier
Early signal, low confidence
Emerging patterns that haven't reached mainstream awareness. Longer edge window—48 hours or more. Requires conviction.
Discovery
Non-obvious connections
Signals that connect seemingly unrelated events into a predictive thesis. Variable timing. The highest value when validated.
