Universities grapple with redefining education amid surge of generative AI tools

18 February 2026  ·  Noah Wire

The rapid spread of generative AI like ChatGPT has led universities to rethink curriculum design, assessment methods, and the core values of higher education, amid increasing pressure to develop responsible AI education.

The rapid diffusion of generative artificial intelligence since the public arrival of ChatGPT has forced schools to confront fundamental questions about pedagogy, assessment and the value of higher education. According to reporting in Columbia Spectator, the arrival of these tools has prompted universities to both embrace AI within curricula and wrestle with how to preserve students' capacities for critical thinking and original work. Empire AI and other regional efforts have further intensified pressure on institutions to define what responsible AI education should look like.
