Investigative Intelligence

It goes where the story is

Agent Noah doesn't wait for news to break. Give it a subject—a company, a council, a market, a person—and it will find everything.

It searches public records, social media feeds, government databases, council meeting minutes, corporate filings, and news archives. Then it assembles what it finds into structured intelligence you can act on.

This is proper investigative journalism, powered by the same narrative signal engine that drives our wires and prediction systems.

No Complex UI

Just tell it what you need

Agent Noah is fully agentic. There's no dashboard to learn, no filters to configure, no queries to build. You describe what you need in plain language. The agent does the rest.

It plans its own investigation strategy, determines which sources to check, cross-references what it finds, and delivers structured intelligence back to you. It's the investigative journalist that never sleeps.
