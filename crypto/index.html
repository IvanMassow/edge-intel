Methodology

How it works

1
Narrative signals detect crypto pressure
Our engine reads thousands of crypto sources daily and publishes directional forecasts: which asset, which direction, and how strong the narrative pressure.
2
Predictions are graded and matched to live markets
Each prediction is scored A–E on source quality, consensus, and momentum. Grade A/B predictions are actively matched against Polymarket and Kalshi contracts. When matched, we paper-trade using our Wave Rider strategy.
3
Signals are scored against ETF benchmarks
Directional calls are tracked against crypto ETF proxies (IBIT for Bitcoin, ETHA for Ethereum). A hit is a >0.5% move in the predicted direction within the scored horizon (6, 12, 24, or 72 hours).
