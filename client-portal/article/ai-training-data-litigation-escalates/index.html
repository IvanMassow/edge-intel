What Practitioners Are Saying

Discovery Collapse and AI Search Shift

Practitioners predominantly describe a pattern where search rankings hold steady but clicks and click-through rates decline significantly after AI Overview expansions. This 'decoupling' of visibility from traffic is observed across SEO communities with concrete dashboard comparisons showing sustained monthly CTR drops. Some report that while click volume drops, conversion quality may hold or improve, creating tension over how severe the impact truly is. Publisher and editor communities were quieter in public forums, suggesting key discussions may be occurring in private or niche channels.

Creator Economy Tooling and Platform Partnerships

Evidence of multi-homing among newsletter creators is common; operators often run both Substack and Beehiiv, weighing trade-offs between revenue cuts and SaaS fees. Debates around the value and earnings reliability of ad-network sponsorships persist, with some CEO AMAs acknowledging publisher complaints related to CPMs and payout friction.

Strong operator chatter found on Reddit for SEO and newsletter tooling; practitioner validation from publisher and editor communities remains limited in this cycle. | Confidence: Medium

Independent Analysis

Independent SEO analytics firms frame AI Overviews' impact as dependent on both AI Overview presence and whether publishers are cited, with studies showing severe CTR declines particularly for non-cited entities. Broader shifts in user behaviour beyond AI features also contribute to decreased clicks on traditional blue links.

Comparative creator posts describe platform switching driven by unit economics and monetisation tooling availability rather than pure preference. However, some case studies on platforms like Beehiiv may reflect promotional bias and should be interpreted cautiously.
