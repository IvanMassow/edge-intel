EverQuest launches Deep MakeTech EIS fund to accelerate UK scale-ups with CPI partnership

By FTS Briefing Newsroom · 47 days ago

EverQuest Capital Partners unveils a new EIS fund targeting advanced sectors like materials, energy, and life sciences The fund leverages CPI’s infrastructure for technical diligence and commercial validation Aims for 3–5x returns within five years, focusing on proven technologies with clear scaling routes

  1. Body Copy

EverQuest Capital Partners has launched EverQuest EIS Fund II, a UK Enterprise Investment Scheme vehicle aimed at later-stage venture and scale-up companies developing advanced processes, materials and design technologies , the sector the manager calls “Deep MakeTech”. The fund emphasises investments in businesses with demonstrable commercial momentum and clear routes to scale and exit.

Target sectors include advanced manufacturing, life sciences, semiconductors, sustainable materials, energy, automotive, agriculture and defence, with capital to be deployed into companies where technology risk has been substantially de‑risked and commercial scale is in sight. EverQuest says the Fund is structured to qualify for EIS tax reliefs, subject to HMRC rules and individual circumstances.

A central pillar of Fund II is its partnership with the Centre for Process Innovation (CPI). According to CPI and industry reports, the collaboration gives EverQuest privileged access to CPI’s technical infrastructure, pilot‑scale facilities and a pipeline of off‑market opportunities developed from more than two decades of industrial engagement. CPI’s role is presented as a source of deep technical diligence and pathways to commercial validation that can accelerate scale‑up and exit readiness.

EverQuest points to the team’s prior track record , cited in public materials as delivering cumulative realised exits in excess of £1bn and specific high‑multiple outcomes from CPI‑related investments , to support the Fund’s focus on disciplined entry pricing, active portfolio support and early exit preparation. Target returns are stated at roughly 3–5x invested capital over a three‑to‑five year horizon, with a concentrated, high‑conviction portfolio construction and hands‑on value creation.

Governance and distribution arrangements are highlighted as part of the proposition: EverQuest will work alongside Kin Capital, which manages significant venture assets for retail and institutional clients, to provide oversight and disciplined execution. The firm also positions Fund II as complementary to broader EIS activity in the UK growth capital market rather than a speculative vehicle, stressing investor suitability considerations given the high‑risk, illiquid nature of EIS investments.

The launch comes amid a wider expansion of EIS‑style funds focused on technology‑led growth companies; other managers have recently introduced comparable offerings targeting mid‑ and deep‑tech opportunities, underscoring continued investor appetite for specialist EIS allocations. EverQuest and CPI say the combination of capital, industrial expertise and scale‑up capability is intended to help move proven technologies towards commercial reality.

Stephane Mery, Partner at EverQuest Capital Partners, said in the company announcement: “The UK has an exceptional base of science and engineering talent, but scaling complex industrial technologies requires more than capital alone. Fund II reflects our belief that combining investment discipline with CPI’s technical depth and infrastructure creates a powerful platform for long term value creation.” Peter Smith, Partner at EverQuest Capital Partners, added: “EverQuest EIS Fund II builds on everything we have learned through Fund I and our work alongside CPI. By investing at the point where technology risk is better understood and commercial scale is in sight, we believe we can support exceptional businesses while offering investors access to high quality EIS opportunities.” Hannah Wade, MD of CPI Enterprises and Partner of EverQuest, said: “CPI’s partnership with EverQuest brings together capital, industrial expertise and scale up capability. We work with many of the world’s leading companies, and through EverQuest EIS Fund II we are pleased to continue supporting innovative businesses as they move from proven technology to commercial reality.”
