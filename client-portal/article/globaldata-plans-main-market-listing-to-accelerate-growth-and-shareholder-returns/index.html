GlobalData plans Main Market listing to accelerate growth and shareholder returns

By FTS Briefing Newsroom · 46 days ago

GlobalData to move from AIM to the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange in March 2026 Robust financials with £322 million revenue and 34% EBITDA margin forecast for 2025 Share buybacks and acquisitions underpin strategic shift and growth ambitions

Body Copy

GlobalData Plc has confirmed plans to shift its listing from AIM to the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange, with the board expecting Admission to the Main Market at 8.00am on 5 March 2026, coinciding with the publication of its annual results on 2 March. Investegate reported the timing and the company’s formal update on the proposed move. [4], [5]

The company said trading for the financial year to 31 December 2025 remained robust amid a period of significant transformation, with expected revenue of approximately £322 million , reported growth of 13% and c.1% on an organic underlying basis. Adjusted EBITDA is forecast at around £110 million, with margins of about 34%, and the group continues to expect margins to return towards 40% in 2026. These figures were set out in the firm’s trading update. [5], [6]

GlobalData’s balance sheet and capital allocation actions have underpinned the market transition. The group expects net debt of roughly £110 million after returning more than £110 million through share buybacks during the year and spending in excess of £40 million on acquisitions, and it reported more than £200 million of available facilities at 31 December 2025. Investing.com and Investegate provided detail on the buyback activity and available facilities. [2], [5]

The board last year signalled its Main Market intention in February 2025 and reconfirmed it in July 2025; a prospectus is expected to be published in due course and, subject to approvals, Admission was earlier expected before the end of 2025 but has been scheduled for March 2026. Reuters-style filings on Investegate and prior announcements detail the timeline adjustments. [4], [3]

Investors have seen a sequence of capital returns: a £60 million tender offer in September 2025, c.£39.7 million repurchases in the first half of the year, and a fresh £10 million buyback programme announced alongside the Main Market plans, part of management’s focus on shareholder returns while investing in AI-enabled products to boost client engagement. Investing.com and company disclosures summarised these programmes and the Capital Markets Event. [2], [6]

Market analysts point to the company’s improved underlying subscription momentum and contributions from recent acquisitions as drivers of growth, though some guidance on second-half margins was tempered during 2025. The firm’s longer-term targets remain ambitious: management has previously outlined growth aspirations and a push towards higher margins as the business scales. Market commentary and prior annual reporting provide context for those objectives. [6], [7]
