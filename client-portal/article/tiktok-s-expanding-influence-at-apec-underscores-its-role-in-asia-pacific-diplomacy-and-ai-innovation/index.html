TikTok's expanding influence at APEC underscores its role in Asia-Pacific diplomacy and AI innovation

By FTS Briefing Newsroom · 89 days ago

TikTok showcased its regional diplomatic and economic impact at the APEC summit in South Korea Ongoing US-China negotiations could reshape TikTok's operations amid geopolitical tensions ByteDance invests heavily in AI to reinforce its multimedia dominance and future growth prospects

TikTok has emerged as a prominent player at the heart of digital diplomacy and commerce in the Asia-Pacific region, as underscored by its high-profile presence at the October 2025 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in South Korea. The platform’s branding was ubiquitous throughout the event, which is considered one of the region’s most significant diplomatic gatherings. Showcasing the platform’s global reach, TikTok executives highlighted its capacity to generate billions in revenue across Asia and its role in fostering a "trusted digital ecosystem." Delegates received TikTok-branded merchandise, and content creators praised the platform for connecting them to hundreds of millions of viewers, illustrating TikTok's critical cultural and economic influence in the region.

While TikTok's onstage prominence at the summit painted a picture of growth and innovation, a parallel narrative unfolded nearby in Busan, where U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping engaged in pivotal discussions. Central to these talks was the future of TikTok’s U.S. operations amid longstanding American concerns over data privacy and Beijing’s influence over the app’s algorithm. These fears culminated in legislation threatening to remove TikTok from U.S. app stores unless its Chinese owner, ByteDance, divested its operations. In response, a consortium of American investors agreed to take over TikTok’s U.S. arm, a move tentatively endorsed by China, signaling a potential resolution to the ongoing geopolitical impasse surrounding the platform.

ByteDance, the Beijing-based parent company, is a rare success story in Chinese tech , having grown from a news aggregator app into a global behemoth now valued at hundreds of billions of dollars. It reached an estimated $400 billion valuation at one point, buoyed by TikTok’s worldwide user base exceeding 1.5 billion monthly active users. Financial reports suggest ByteDance earns revenues upward of $155 billion annually, generating significant profit with strong international income streams, including an estimated $15 billion from U.S. markets alone. The company plans a U.S.-based joint venture to manage TikTok operations domestically, where ByteDance would retain a minority stake. However, questions remain over the control of TikTok’s algorithm, with indications the core algorithmic technology may remain under ByteDance’s ownership, potentially involving licensing fees that could siphon substantial profits back to China.

Beyond social media, ByteDance is aggressively positioning itself at the forefront of artificial intelligence innovation. Following the debut of its Doubao chatbot, which now boasts 157 million monthly active users in China, ByteDance is leveraging AI to enhance content creation on TikTok and its video editing app, CapCut. Its AI innovations have earned recognition within the global research community, including accolades for efficient image generation techniques. Despite ByteDance not leading in traditional large language models (LLMs), a domain dominated by rivals like DeepSeek, Alibaba, and Moonshot AI, its strength lies in interactive and visual AI, demonstrating the company's strategic focus on multimedia applications.

The competitive AI landscape in China has intensified, especially following the breakthrough of DeepSeek's compact yet powerful model earlier this year. This event spurred major tech players like Alibaba, Baidu, and others to accelerate their AI development efforts, embracing open-source releases to foster wider experimentation. ByteDance’s response includes heavy investments in cutting-edge Nvidia chips, the company reportedly being the largest purchaser within China, and exploring custom processor designs. These technology investments are coupled with substantial capital expenditures planned for 2025, though the company remains private and thus reliant on internal financing mechanisms, unlike public giants such as Alibaba and Alphabet.

TikTok’s influence extends well beyond technology, with significant cultural and economic ramifications in the Asia-Pacific. At the APEC summit, TikTok's senior director for public policy Eric Ebenstein spotlighted the platform’s instrumental role in popularising Korean culture globally, citing phenomena such as the viral spread of "gimbap" leading to nationwide sellouts in U.S. markets and a Korean brand topping sales on TikTok Shop stateside. Ebenstein also underscored TikTok’s commitment to creating a safer, more inclusive digital space through collaborations with Korean organizations focused on youth protection and digital literacy. The company views the Asia-Pacific as a dynamic hub for innovation and creativity, underlining the platform's broader vision of empowering regional entrepreneurs and cultural exports.

On a broader regional scale, South Korea’s President Lee Jae Myung used the APEC summit to advocate for collective action among member states to harness AI's potential while addressing demographic challenges such as population aging and low birth rates. He introduced the APEC AI Initiative with a focus on turning these societal shifts into growth opportunities through collaboration. The summit also saw key dialogues on aligning AI strategies with economic and social policies, reflecting Asia-Pacific’s vital role as a centre of global trade and GDP, which further contextualizes TikTok’s significance within this geopolitical and technological nexus.

While ByteDance navigates regulatory and market uncertainties, the resolution of its U.S. operation sale could unlock new financial resources and strategic focus, potentially enabling a long-anticipated initial public offering (IPO). This prospective liquidity event offers ByteDance a pathway to fund escalating AI ambitions and maintain competitiveness in both global social media and artificial intelligence arenas. Yet, as the company continues its quiet but calculated approach to innovation, the evolving dynamics around TikTok’s governance and technological development remain a critical episode in the interplay between tech, geopolitics, and culture.
